1. The short answer

For-profit status is the big filter. Most government grant money for these populations goes only to nonprofits, towns, or housing authorities. The money P.A.T.H. Operation can actually get comes in three forms, and none of them is a free check:

  1. Operating contracts and per-diem rates: DDS, DCF, BSAS, Medicaid waivers. This is the big recurring money, and in most states for-profits are allowed.
  2. 0% deferred capital loans: MA FCF, NH OUD Capital. These work almost like grants because no payments are due as long as the housing stays affordable.
  3. Getting paid as a landlord by voucher holders: FUP, FYI, CHAP, RAP, MRVP.

To get true grant money (CoC, HIF, CCRI, Section 811 capital, USDA), P.A.T.H. Operation needs a 501(c)(3) partner or sister nonprofit. The nonprofit holds the grant and leases the property from the LLC, at arm's length.

⏰ Deadlines coming up:
  • Sept 24, 2026 (2 days away): MA Winter 2027 rental round pre-application. This round covers HSF, AHTF and LIHTC, and for-profits are eligible. The full application is due Dec 3, 2026. ⚠
  • Sept 30, 2026: HUD CoC federal deadline. This one is for nonprofits and governments only, and local CoC deadlines have almost certainly passed.
  • Now: RI BHDDH recovery housing RFP (about $4M), which was expected in summer 2026. Check whether RI Purchasing has released it. ⚠

5. Recommended next moves

  1. Decide this week about the MA Winter round: pre-application due 9/24, full application 12/3. This only makes sense if P.A.T.H. Operation has a 5+ unit project ready.
  2. Set up a sister 501(c)(3) or line up a nonprofit partner. It opens CoC, HIF, CCRI, Section 811 capital, USDA and most opioid money.
  3. IDD track: MA (DDS contract + FCF), ME (Section 21 direct enrollment), CT (DDS Qualified Provider).
  4. Recovery track: get certified first (MASH, RICARES, CTARR, NHCORR, VTARR, MARR). Certification is what unlocks every recovery dollar.
  5. Aged-out youth track: be the landlord for FUP/FYI/CHAP/VEC youth now, and watch COMMBUYS for the next MA DCF young-adult procurement.
  6. Pull the rate tables by hand: MA 101 CMR 420 / 427 / 346, the CT CLA rates by level of need, the RI OHIC 2025 IDD benchmarks, and the ME Section 21 T2016 rate.
